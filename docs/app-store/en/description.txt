Today's workout, ready in five seconds.

WorkoutKit is a focused fitness app that auto-generates today's training plan from four simple choices: your goal, the muscles you want to train, the equipment you can use, and the time you have. No subscriptions. No ads. Your data stays on your device.


■ Highlights

• 345 exercises bundled, fully translated in Japanese and English
• Anatomical illustration of target muscles, split into Front and Back sections
• Step-by-step form cues + common mistakes + cautions for every exercise
• Live Activity support — track progress and rest timers from the lock screen and Dynamic Island
• Set logging (weight / reps / RPE / rest seconds) stored locally with SwiftData
• Rest timer with sound + haptic feedback
• History list, calendar view, persistent sort order
• 1RM (Epley formula) calculator
• kg / lbs toggle, follows the system language (Japanese / English)
• iPhone and iPad (NavigationSplitView on iPad)
• Light and dark appearance


■ The Builder wizard

Pick a goal (hypertrophy / strength / fat loss / endurance / full body) → target muscles → available equipment → time budget. WorkoutKit composes the optimal session in one tap. Use Shuffle for variety or Choose to hand-pick exercises. Warm-up, main work, and cool-down are placed automatically.


■ The Library

All 345 exercises filterable by type × muscle × equipment × mechanics. Each detail page shows:
- Anatomical highlight of the primary and secondary muscles
- Numbered form cues placed on the front and back silhouettes
- Step-by-step instructions
- Common mistakes
- Safety cautions

A reference you can return to as you learn new lifts.
